Sales Data Analysis

This Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard provides a comprehensive overview of business outcomes across key metrics, including revenue, cost, profit, customer base, and product performance. The organization generated ₦3M in total sales against ₦2M in total cost, resulting in a profit of ₦932K from 250 customers, indicating strong operational efficiency. Profit contribution varies by brand, with Apple leading at ₦197K, followed by Lenovo and Samsung at ₦160K each, while Dell records the lowest profit at ₦126K. Profit is also well distributed across customer income segments, led by medium-income customers contributing ₦347K (37.23%), followed by high-income (₦302K, 32.43%) and low-income customers (₦283K, 30.34%).

Sales performance insights further reveal customer preferences and market trends. Product color analysis shows White products dominate sales at ₦1,218K, ahead of Gray (₦1,030K) and Black (₦858K). However, yearly sales show a declining pattern from ₦1,152K in 2020 to ₦991K in 2021 and ₦963K in 2022, suggesting emerging market challenges or shifting demand. Despite this decline, monthly customer activity remains stable, with 1.74K customers in January, 1.72K in February, and 1.74K in March, indicating consistent engagement. The dashboard also includes regional filters (East, North, South, and West), enabling targeted geographic analysis and data-driven decision-making.
